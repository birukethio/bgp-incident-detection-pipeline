# Chapter 5 Findings Handoff v1

Key findings: K1 generalized strongly on untouched Holdout, Top-2 ranking was high, Outage was strongest, Hijack was hardest, and Stage 1 remained unresolved. Limitations include RouteViews observability, external labels, post-Validation K1 development interpretation, and finite Holdout size. Future work should prioritize Normal/Stage 1, shorter-window inference, enterprise-local telemetry, and calibrated ranking.
