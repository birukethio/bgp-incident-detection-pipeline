K1-B0 Mean Probability vs K1 Temporal Stacking

## Figure 4.10 Subtitle
Post-Validation TRAIN-only nested-CV comparison of incident-level aggregation strategies.

## Key Text Under Figure
Temporal stacking increased incident Macro F1 by 0.131729, Top-1 by 0.129909, Top-2 by 0.057402, MRR by 0.074522, and Hijack F1 by 0.163781. The paired grouped-fold artifact reports that the temporal stacker won 5/5 folds versus K1-B0.
