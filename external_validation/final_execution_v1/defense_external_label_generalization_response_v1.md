The original labels were primarily Cloudflare-derived, so I added a prospectively frozen, source-separated validation of the reproducibly preserved Level-1 path. Thirty-seven non-Cloudflare candidates were screened without model output; 18 independently sourced incidents passed fixed timestamp, overlap, pilot-exclusion, and archive gates and were locked before inference. The frozen five-model Level-1 ensemble achieved Macro F1 0.412698 and Top-2 0.722222 on those 18 incidents. This is supportive but bounded evidence: only three Hijacks were included, IODA supplied ten Outages, and it does not validate the historical Level-2 stacker.
